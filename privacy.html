Privacy Policy — Artho (অৰ্থ অভিধান)

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Artho is an English–Assamese dictionary app by Saurabh Saha. This policy explains what the app does with your information — in short: nothing leaves your device except dictionary lookups.

What the app collects

Nothing. Artho does not create accounts, does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Internet use

Word meanings are fetched from the public XOBDO dictionary API. When you search a word, only that search word is sent to the API to retrieve its meaning. No identifier, location, or personal data is attached to these requests.

On-device storage

Your favorites, search history, and text-size preference are stored only on your own device (in the app's private storage). They are never transmitted anywhere and are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Artho requests only the Internet permission, needed to fetch dictionary meanings. It requests no access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
